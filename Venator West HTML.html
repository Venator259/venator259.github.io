The Three Territories

Where We Are Going

I
Mount Rainier
Washington · Sept 9–11
A 14,411-foot active volcano draped in glaciers. Alpine meadows, wildflower trails, and views that stop you mid-sentence. Paradise, it turns out, lives up to its name.
II
Olympic
Washington · Sept 12–18
Three ecosystems in one park — temperate rainforest, rugged Pacific coastline, and glacier-capped peaks. The Greenwood has nothing on the Hoh. The most diverse national park in the country.
III
North Cascades
Washington · Sept 18–19
America's Alps. More glaciers than any park outside Alaska. Turquoise lakes from glacial flour. Larch trees turning gold in September — which, unlike certain other golden rings, is entirely benign.

Legendary Encounters

What You Cannot Miss

♨️
Sol Duc Hot Springs
Natural mineral pools steps from camp, 98–107°F. Four nights in a row. After a day of hiking, this is everything. Tolwyn does not have to sacrifice anything to use them.
🛳
Victoria, BC
A 90-minute ferry crossing to Canada. British colonial harbour town, fresh seafood, and the kind of afternoon you'll talk about for years. Passports required. No attainder recognised.
🌿
Hoh Rainforest
Moss-draped maple canopy, 140 inches of rain a year, Roosevelt Elk on the trail. One of the most otherworldly places in North America. Wren is wouldn't to keep it together. Mostly.
🏔
Washington Pass
Standing at the edge of a cliff looking at jagged granite spires and glaciers with the larches going gold below. One of the great views in the American West. No druid surveillance detected. Or noticed?
🌊
Rialto Beach
Sea stacks, tide pools, driftwood logs the size of houses, and the raw North Pacific. Not a Florida beach. Considerably better. No irrigation ditches. No serpents. Probably.
🏛
Lake Quinault Lodge
A 1926 National Historic Landmark on a glassy rainforest lake. FDR slept here. You will too. After twelve days of camping, the dining room will feel like Stigandr gave you the best table in the house.
The Compendium

Need to Know

Passport
Required for Victoria BC on Sept 15. No passport, no Canada. This is not negotiable. Unlike most things in Venator, there is no workaround.
Weather
Days 55–70°F, nights drop to the low 40s. Olympic coast gets fog and rain regardless of forecast. Pack a real rain jacket and warm layers. The Greenwood is not the only thing that watches from the cold.
Campsites
All campgrounds are pre-reserved. Flush toilets and running water at every site. Unlike the Sylvan Marches, nobody has to dig anything.
Cell Service
Minimal to none inside all three parks. Download offline maps before you leave. Tell someone your itinerary. No Expeditious Messengers will reach you here. This is a feature.
The Vehicle
Rolling in a Toyota Grand Highlander. Three rows, everyone has room, gear fits. Nobody is getting ambushed in a ditch from this vehicle. Probably.
Park Pass
America the Beautiful Pass covers all three national parks — $80 total, split four ways. Unlike House Medani contracts, this one is simple and the terms are clear.
